# Skanītis — Burvju spēles

Small learning games for children, in Latvian and English. Live at [skanitis.lv](https://skanitis.lv/).

The site is plain HTML pages with no server and no build step, so everything runs in the browser. Settings, stars and recent words are saved only in that browser, and your own typing text is never uploaded.

## Games

- **Burtiņu Burvis (Letter Wizard).** A typing game with short words, fairytale sentences or your own text (for example a poem or a school text). It has three difficulty levels and collects a star for every correct letter.
- **Ciparu Burvis (Number Wizard).** Practice for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, with three levels and a choice of 5, 10 or 20 problems.

## The sky

Every page shares the same animated background ([sky.js](sky.js)), and it follows the real time of day in Latvia:

- **Day** (sunrise to sunset): a pastel sky with the sun, slow clouds, floating blossoms and the odd flock of birds.
- **Night**: twinkling stars, the moon in its real current phase, and now and then a satellite, the space station or a plane passing by.
- The sun and moon move along a gentle arc to where they would be right now, and they sit in the same spot on every page.

The 🌙/☀️ button switches between day and night, and 🎵 turns on quiet background music. 🪄 changes the colours, and 🌐 switches the language.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser.
